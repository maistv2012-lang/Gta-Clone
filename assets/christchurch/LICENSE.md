# Cathedral Square demonstration assets

The PBR texture pixels in this folder are original procedural work generated by
`scripts/generate-city-textures.py`, using deterministic noise and masonry/paving
patterns. They do not contain pixels from the user's reference image or any
third-party photograph. These original texture outputs are dedicated to the
public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Building, cathedral, street-furniture, vegetation, character and vehicle geometry
is authored in the project source; no downloaded model or model inferred from a
single photograph is used. Third-party engine/addons remain Three.js r170, MIT,
with their license in vendor/three/LICENSE. OSM footprints, paths and coordinates
remain © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, with attribution preserved.

The Cathedral is an approximate, game-ready reconstruction from its OSM footprint
and architectural forms, shown with restoration scaffolding because the bundled
OSM record marks ruins/access=no. Ornament, dimensions not supplied by OSM, props,
facades and vegetation placements are artistic approximations, not survey data.
No official police endorsement or trademarked vehicle manufacturer is implied.

Original geometry generated by the new city-detailing and vehicle-detail modules
and the new character rig is also offered as CC0 visual asset data. This does not
relicense the pre-existing game code, Three.js, OSM data or third-party software.
